Procurement lead time vs. the schedule window actually available before energization, per long-lead package — a negative buffer is the package driving (or about to drive) the completion date, a signal worth surfacing before it shows up as a bridge-step variance after the fact.

real Substation transformer lead times have stretched from roughly 140 weeks in 2023 to 160+ weeks in 2026 (power transformers averaging 128 weeks, generator step-up units 144 weeks per Wood Mackenzie's Q2 2025 survey), with the largest high-voltage units running up to ~200+ weeks (nearly 4 years) — corroborated across Turner & Townsend's 2025-2026 index and independent 2026 industry reporting (Build.inc, PowerMag, Terrapin Consulting Group). Contingency Drawdown & AACE RP 57R-09 Monte Carlo Band real method

Assigns Beta-PERT/Triangular/Log-Normal probability distributions (optimistic/most-likely/pessimistic bounds) to WBS cost and duration line items, then runs many simulated draws to produce a cumulative distribution curve — P50 (median), P80, P90 confidence thresholds for sizing contingency reserves, instead of a single-point estimate that hides the real range of outcomes.

An elevated drawdown ratio early in a project is itself the early-warning signal. The P50/P80/P90 band follows AACE International RP 57R-09 — a real, named industry practice.
