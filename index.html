A cost-management methodology demo built around a synthetic capital-development program — 11 tabs, real math computed live in this page's own JavaScript, no build step, no server, no dependency to install.

Contingency Drawdown & AACE RP 57R-09 Monte Carlo Band real method

Assigns Beta-PERT/Triangular/Log-Normal probability distributions (optimistic/most-likely/pessimistic bounds) to WBS cost and duration line items, then runs many simulated draws to produce a cumulative distribution curve — P50 (median), P80, P90 confidence thresholds for sizing contingency reserves, instead of a single-point estimate that hides the real range of outcomes.

An elevated drawdown ratio early in a project is itself the early-warning signal. The P50/P80/P90 band follows AACE International RP 57R-09 — a real, named industry practice.
